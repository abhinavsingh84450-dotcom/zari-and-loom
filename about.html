The Story of Zari & Loom

Preserving India’s Living Textile Heirlooms

Founded in Sector 12, Dwarka, Zari & Loom is born out of profound reverence for India’s pit looms, generational weavers, and the sacred ritual of bridal trousseaus. We eliminate middlemen to connect families directly with the authentic heartbeat of Indian handlooms.

Our Genesis

Where Ancient Weaving Meets Modern Brides

In an era dominated by synthetic power-loom imitations and fleeting fast fashion, Zari & Loom was conceived as an unyielding sanctuary for slow, intentional craftsmanship. We recognized that brides in Delhi NCR sought more than just a wedding outfit—they sought an heirloom woven with genuine human prayer, patience, and purity.

Every saree in our studio is born from weeks, and often months, of focused hand-throwing on wooden looms. By establishing direct community pacts with master weaver families, we honor fair living wages and revive vanishing historic weaves—from ancient kadwa meenakari to korvai interlocked borders.

“A real handloom saree does not merely drape a bride; it enfolds her in centuries of artisan memory, devotion, and ancestral blessings.”
— Founders, Zari & Loom Dwarka

Artisan Guilds

Direct Partnerships with India’s Master Loom Centers

We work intimately with cooperative guilds and generational master craftsmen across India’s most celebrated weaving clusters.

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Banarasi Kadwa & Katan Masters

Collaborating with third-generation weavers along the ghats of Varanasi. Our artisans specialize in the rare Kadwa technique—where every floral buta is individually hand-engraved with silver electroplated zari without any loose threads on the reverse.

  • Heritage: Over 400 years of pit loom lineage
  • Signatures: Rangkat, Shikargah, Jangla, Katan Pure Silk
  • Artisan Impact: Sustaining 42 weaver families with pension reserves
Chanderi, Madhya Pradesh

Gossamer Silk-Cotton Weavers

In the heart of Bundelkhand, our Chanderi guild weaves gossamer-weight fabrics by blending high-twist degummed mulberry silk with natural combed cotton. They execute micro Ashrafi and peacock booties using ancient wooden dobby mechanisms.

  • Heritage: Royal patronage dating back to the Scindia dynasty
  • Signatures: Eknaliya borders, Tissue Drapes, Gold Zari Pallus
  • Artisan Impact: Fair-wage cotton procurement and loom upgrades
Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu

Korvai Temple Silk Craftsmen

Sourced from the temple city of thousand looms, our Kanjeevarams are woven with double warp and three-ply twisted mulberry silk. Master weavers employ the dual-weaver Korvai technique to interlock heavy contrast borders seamlessly.

  • Heritage: Sacred temple motif traditions (Yali, Mayil, Rudraksha)
  • Signatures: Heavy Contrast Borders, Pure Gold-Wash Zari
  • Artisan Impact: Direct weaver-to-studio procurement with zero broker fees
Uncompromising Purity

The Silk Mark & Pure Zari Authenticity Guarantee

When you invest in a bridal saree at Zari & Loom, you receive absolute material certitude. We subject every batch to stringent quality verifications.

✓

100% Certified Silk Mark Tag

Each pure silk creation bears an individualized barcode tag from the Silk Mark Organisation of India (SMOI), guaranteeing genuine natural silk protein fibers.

✓

Real Electroplated Zari Verification

We never use cheap plastic metallic yarns (Lurex). Our bridal zaris feature genuine silver electroplated copper or silver-gilt thread that matures with a warm, regal patina.

✓

Certified Handloom Mark Verification

Our weaves carry the Government of India Handloom Mark, certifying that the drape was woven manually on traditional pit or frame looms.

♦

Our Purity Certificate

Every bridal saree purchased at our studio is accompanied by our formal Certificate of Authenticity, detailing:

  • Exact Weaver Guild & Origin Village
  • Warp & Weft Silk Thread Counts
  • Zari Metallurgical Composition
  • Individual Loom Number & Weaver Signature
  • Lifelong Preservation & Muslin Wrapping Guide
