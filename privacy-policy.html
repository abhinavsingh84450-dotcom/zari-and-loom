Legal Transparency & Data Ethics

Client Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, Meta Advertising Standards, and Google Analytics 4 Guidelines.

Detailed Privacy Framework

Our Sacred Privacy Pledge

At Zari & Loom (Dwarka, New Delhi), we honor your personal privacy with the same fidelity we dedicate to authentic handlooms. We collect only the information strictly necessary to facilitate your bridal styling appointments and weave consultations. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties.

1. Data Fiduciary & Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected by Zari & Loom (“we”, “us”, or “our”), acting as the Data Fiduciary under the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India).

Our registered flagship studio is located at: Plot 14, Sector 12, Dwarka, New Delhi, Delhi 110078, India. This policy governs all personal data collected through our website (www.zariandloom.com), bridal consultation enquiry forms, WhatsApp Business communications, and digital advertising campaigns (including Meta Lead Ads and Google Ads).

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal data strictly when you voluntarily submit it to us or when you interact with our online boutique:

  • Appointment & Enquiry Details: Your full name, telephone number (with SMS/WhatsApp capability), email address, preferred appointment date, wedding ceremony date, occasion type (e.g., Bridal Trousseau, Sangeet, Festive Gifting), and specific weave preferences (e.g., Banarasi Kadwa, Chanderi, Kanjeevaram).
  • Direct Communications: Notes recorded during telephone calls, studio visits, or WhatsApp conversations regarding blouse color coordination, border measurements, and custom embroidery requirements.
  • Automated Technical Data: Anonymized device metrics, browser type, geographic region (city level), referral URLs, and session interaction timings collected automatically via Google Analytics 4.

3. Purpose and Legal Grounds for Processing (DPDP Act Compliance)

Under the Indian DPDP Act, we process your personal data only for lawful purposes based on your explicit consent:

Data Category Purpose of Processing Legal Basis
Contact & Bridal Details Scheduling private bridal appointments, confirming bookings via phone or WhatsApp, and coordinating bespoke drapes. Explicit Consent via Form Submission
Inquiry Message Content Preparing curated saree selections and color palettes tailored to your bridal trousseau. Contractual / Legitimate Request
Technical & Analytics Data Monitoring website speed, preventing fraudulent submissions, and optimizing mobile browsing. Legitimate Interest (Anonymized)

4. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Cookie Disclosure

Our website utilizes Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analysis service provided by Google LLC. GA4 employs first-party cookies to evaluate how visitors navigate our collections and consultation pages.

  • IP Anonymization: In compliance with data protection best practices, IP addresses are processed in an anonymized manner and are not permanently logged or attributable to individual users.
  • Cookie Control: You can configure your browser to decline cookies, block tracking scripts, or alert you when cookies are being sent. Our website remains fully functional and accessible even if cookies are disabled.
  • No Cross-Site Profiling: We do not connect GA4 analytical metrics with personally identifiable appointment submissions.

5. Meta Lead Ads & Digital Advertising Transparency

To present our handloom heritage to wedding families in Delhi NCR, we periodically operate digital campaigns on Meta platforms (Instagram and Facebook):

  • Meta Lead Ads: When you submit an enquiry form through an Instagram or Facebook advertisement for Zari & Loom, your information (name, phone, bridal date) is transferred directly into our secure consultation registry. It is used exclusively by our Dwarka studio draping consultants to follow up on your enquiry.
  • Meta Pixel: We may use the Meta Pixel to evaluate the effectiveness of our advertising by measuring actions visitors take on our website. You can manage your ad preferences or opt out of personalized advertisements via your Meta Account Settings (Meta Ad Preferences).

6. Data Retention & Security Protocols

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption: All transmissions between your browser and our website are encrypted using 256-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS).
  • Restricted Studio Access: Your contact details are accessible solely by authorized senior bridal consultants and our studio management.
  • Retention Period: Inquiry data is retained for a maximum of 24 months to support your wedding planning, anniversary trousseau maintenance, and saree care support, after which it is permanently purged or anonymized.

7. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal residing in India or visiting our boutique, you are entitled to exercise the following statutory rights under the DPDP Act:

Right to Access

Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.

Right to Correction

Request correction or updating of any inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete contact information.

Right to Erasure

Request complete deletion of your personal records from our enquiry databases and communication logs.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Revoke your consent for consultation outreach or WhatsApp messaging at any moment without penalty.
